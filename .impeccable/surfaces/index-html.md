## Scope and mode

Redesign of the live GitHub Pages tool in `index.html`. Visitor mode: operate.

## Audience, job, and action

Arabic typographers, designers, developers, and readers choose from a compact test corpus or paste plain text, switch between prose and poetry composition, choose a kashida-placement style, alter the measure and type size, inspect the fitted proof, and copy the unchanged source.

## Proof and constraints

The live transformation is the proof. Preserve the source string and line breaks, `simple` as the default, opt-in `naskh` and `nastaliq`, responsive operation, keyboard access, and the dependency-free static stack. The poetry sample uses two source lines per verse and the real `layoutArabicPoem()` adapter; prose, vocalized Arabic, Persian, and mixed-script samples use `justifyArabic()`. Do not invent usage or performance claims.

## Chosen direction

Interactive Arabic type specimen: cool proof paper, near-black ink, ultramarine measurement marks, a foundry grid, square controls, and Arabic text at dominant scale. The memorable moment is the source becoming a measured typographic proof between blue rails.
